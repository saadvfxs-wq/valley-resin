08 — Questions

Good to
know.

Still deciding? Send a message with a photo of the room and we'll tell you what's possible.

Ask us anything
How long does a resin floor last?
A properly prepared and sealed resin floor is a 15–20 year surface in a home. We specify the build to the traffic it will take, so a busy commercial floor gets a heavier system than a bedroom.
Is a metallic floor slippery?
A gloss metallic floor can be slick when wet. We offer satin seals and anti-slip additives, and we'll recommend what suits the room rather than what looks best in a photo.
Can it go over my existing floor?
Usually yes — concrete, tile or screed, provided it is sound and dry. We test moisture and adhesion before quoting so there are no surprises once the crew arrives.
How do I look after it?
Damp mop with a pH-neutral cleaner. No steam, no abrasive pads. We leave a simple care guide and a small touch-up kit with every installation.
Do you travel outside California?
Yes. We regularly take on projects worldwide and work with trusted local partners for logistics and site support. Travel is itemised transparently in the quote.
What does it cost?
Metallic systems typically start around $12–$18 per sq ft installed, depending on area, substrate condition and finish. Your quote is fixed once we've seen the site.
